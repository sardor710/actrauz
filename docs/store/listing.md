### Full description
```
Actra is a sports community and venue-booking app for Uzbekistan and Kazakhstan. Find a court or a pitch, join a game, or find players to play with — in Tashkent, Almaty and Astana.

What you can do
• Book courts and pitches. Browse venues in your city, see free times and book in a few taps. Pay at the venue.
• Join games. Open games show the sport, time, place and the level of players needed. Join one, or host your own.
• Game Requests. Looking for players? Post a request and let others answer.
• Tournaments. Register with a partner, and if a category is full you can join the waitlist.
• Levels and reliability. Set your level for each sport and play with people of a similar level. A reliability score rewards players who show up.
• Notifications. Booking, game and tournament updates arrive in the in-app inbox.

For venue owners
Owners get a booking calendar for their venue, including walk-in and phone bookings. Actra administrators manage venues, sports, tournaments and announcements.

Languages
Uzbek, Russian, Kazakh and English.

Sign in with Telegram
There are no passwords. You share your phone number with the Actra bot on Telegram and come back to the app. The app asks once for your approximate location, only to suggest the nearest city; the location stays on your phone, and you can choose the city yourself instead.

Privacy
No ads, and we do not sell your data. You can delete your account in the app at any time (Profile → Delete account).

Sports include football, padel, tennis, basketball and volleyball, with more to come.
```
