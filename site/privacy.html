Who we are

Actra is a sports community and venue-booking app for Uzbekistan and Kazakhstan. The operator is <founder: legal entity name>, <founder: postal address>. Contact: <founder: contact e-mail>.

What we collect

  • Account: the phone number you share with the Actra bot on Telegram (@actraapp), your Telegram user id, your name, your chosen city and language, and your level for each sport. There are no passwords and we do not ask for an e-mail address.
  • Activity in the app: bookings, games you join or host, game requests, tournament registrations, your notifications, and reliability events (for example attended or missed games).
  • Staff actions: venue owner and administrator actions are written to an audit log.
  • Location: during onboarding the app asks once for your approximate location, only to suggest the nearest city. The coordinates are used on your device only. They are not sent to our servers and not stored. You can refuse and choose the city yourself.

We do not collect payment data (you pay at the venue), contacts, photos or advertising identifiers. The app has no ads and no analytics or crash-reporting tools. We do not sell your data.

Why we use it

To run your account, to book courts and pitches, to organise games and tournaments, to match players by level, to calculate the reliability score, to send you in-app notifications, and to keep the service secure.

Who can see it

  • Other players see your name, your level and your reliability.
  • The owner of a venue sees your name and phone number for bookings at that venue.
  • Actra administrators can see account and booking data needed to run the service.
  • Our hosting provider, Supabase, processes the data on our behalf.

Where it is stored

Data is stored with Supabase in the region ap-south-1 (Mumbai, India) and is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). <legal review: personal-data localisation rules in Uzbekistan and Kazakhstan>

How long we keep it

We keep your account data while your account exists. When you delete your account, your name, phone number, Telegram account link, levels and notifications are removed. Anonymous booking history stays so that venues keep their records; it no longer shows your name or phone number. Audit-log entries about staff actions are kept. <legal review: backup retention and statutory retention periods>

Deleting your account

In the app: Profile → Delete account. Your upcoming bookings and tournament registrations are cancelled and you leave your games. Administrator accounts must first be demoted. Signing in again with the same phone number creates a new, empty account. Without the app, see how to request deletion.

Your rights and contact

You can ask us what we hold about you, ask for corrections or ask for deletion. Write to <founder: contact e-mail>.

Changes

We will update this page when the app changes (for example when payments or push notifications are added) and change the date above.

Чтобы вести ваш аккаунт, бронировать корты и поля, организовывать игры и турниры, подбирать игроков по уровню, рассчитывать рейтинг надёжности, отправлять уведомления внутри приложения и обеспечивать безопасность сервиса.

Кто это видит

  • Другие игроки видят ваше имя, уровень и надёжность.
  • Владелец площадки видит ваше имя и номер телефона по бронированиям на своей площадке.
  • Администраторы Actra видят данные аккаунтов и бронирований, необходимые для работы сервиса.
  • Наш хостинг-провайдер Supabase обрабатывает данные по нашему поручению.

Как долго мы храним данные

Данные аккаунта хранятся, пока существует аккаунт. При удалении аккаунта ваше имя, номер телефона, привязка к Telegram, уровни и уведомления удаляются. Обезличенная история бронирований остаётся, чтобы у площадок сохранялись записи; в ней больше нет вашего имени и номера. Записи журнала аудита о действиях персонала сохраняются. <юридическая проверка: хранение резервных копий и сроки хранения по закону>

Если снова войти с тем же номером телефона, будет создан новый пустой аккаунт. Без приложения: как запросить удаление.
